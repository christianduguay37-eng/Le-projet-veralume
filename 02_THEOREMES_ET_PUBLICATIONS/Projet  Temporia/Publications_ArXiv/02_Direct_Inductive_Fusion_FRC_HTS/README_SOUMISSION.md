- **Abstract (Texte brut pour le champ Abstract) :**
```text
Conventional magnetic confinement fusion programs (e.g., ITER, DEMO) remain locked into the gigawatt-scale D-T tokamak architecture, which suffers from three fundamental economic and engineering bottlenecks: (i) destructive 14.1 MeV neutron wall loading exceeding 20 dpa/year, (ii) unproven tritium breeding in complex lithium blankets, and (iii) severe thermal efficiency limits ($\eta_{\text{th}} \approx 33\%$) imposed by Rankine steam turbine cycles.

We formulate a complete magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) and electrodynamic framework for compact, high-$\beta$ Field-Reversed Configuration (FRC) plasmoids undergoing periodic inductive compression and expansion directly coupled to High-Temperature Superconducting (HTS) REBCO (Rare-Earth Barium Copper Oxide) flux-compression coils.

Our architecture demonstrates three physical breakthroughs:

1. **Aneutronic Non-Equilibrium Operation ($T_i \gg T_e$):** Operating in a two-temperature collisionless regime ($T_i \sim 100 - 150\text{ keV}$, $T_e \sim 15 - 20\text{ keV}$), electron synchrotron and Bremsstrahlung radiation losses are suppressed by an order of magnitude, enabling net-positive energy production with clean advanced fuels ($p\text{-}^{11}\text{B}$ and closed-loop catalytic $\text{D}\text{-}^3\text{He}$).

2. **Direct Inductive Energy Conversion ($\eta_{\text{dir}} > 65\%$):** As the fusion-heated FRC plasmoid expands against the magnetic confinement field, the high magnetic Reynolds number ($R_m > 10^5$) prevents magnetic field penetration. The expanding diamagnetic boundary forces flux exclusion, inducing high-voltage electromotive force (EMF) directly in the external HTS REBCO windings. This bypasses the thermal Carnot cycle entirely, transforming kinetic plasma pressure directly into utility-scale electricity.

3. **Passive Tilt Stabilization via Superconducting Fluxon Reaction:** The back-EMF induced in the REBCO coils produces an instantaneous, conservative restoring magnetic pressure that dampens the internal $n=1$ rotational tilt instability of the FRC without requiring heavy neutral beam injection.

4. **Resonant Switching via Gallium Arsenide PCSS:** Solid-state Photoconductive Semiconductor Switches operating under Zero-Voltage Switching (ZVS) resonance reduce pulsed power commutation losses by $95\%$.

We define a strict Popperian falsification metric: if the direct inductive recovery efficiency falls below $65\%$ at peak ion temperatures $T_i > 100\text{ keV}$, the underlying electrodynamic coupling is invalidated.
```
