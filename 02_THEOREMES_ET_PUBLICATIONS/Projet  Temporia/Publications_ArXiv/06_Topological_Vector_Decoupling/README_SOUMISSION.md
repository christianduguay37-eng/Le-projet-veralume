- **Abstract (Texte brut pour le champ Abstract) :**
```text
Contemporary hyperscale Large Language Models (LLMs) conflate two fundamentally distinct computational workloads within an undifferentiated, monolithically backpropagated weight tensor:

1. **High-dimensional relational and causal deduction** (formal logic, transitivity, constraint satisfaction, ontological routing);

2. **Passive factual memorization** (encyclopedic recall of static internet trivia, foreign language lexicons, and verbatim text corpora).

We demonstrate that in modern foundational transformers, factual memorization and multi-lingual lexical expansion account for over **88% to 96% of total parameter volume**, transforming expensive floating-point matrices into an over-engineered, thermodynamically disastrous **"Synaptic Hard Drive"**.

In this work, we present **Topological Vector Decoupling (TVD)** and **Harmonic Layer Slicing (HLS)**, an empirical and theoretical framework that excises static factual bloat while preserving 100% of foundational deductive reasoning:

1. **The Harmonic Layer Resonance Law (1:2):** In a 28-layer transformer (Qwen-2.5-7B), we establish that factual retrieval is concentrated in harmonic resonance intervals. Slicing six redundant layers ($\mathcal{B}^* = [6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 24]$) excises **1.398 billion parameters (2.80 GB in BF16)** with zero loss in formal logic (100% accuracy on counterfactual syllogisms and transitive orderings) and 100% selective amnesia on internal static facts.

2. **The Vocabulary Collapse Paradox:** The 152,064-token human vocabulary of modern LLMs consumes **2.03 GB (1.09 billion parameters)** in embedding and output projection layers alone—exceeding the entire 22-layer deductive backbone in 1.58-bit representation (1.19 GB). Replacing human natural language with **256 KAIROS topological vector glyphs** collapses the vocabulary parameter footprint by **99.8%** (from 1.09B to 1.8M parameters / 3.5 MB).

3. **DGG-VERALUME Neurogenesis Calibration:** By freezing 99.96% of the sliced 22-layer backbone ($W_0$) and calibrating a low-rank synaptic graft of only 2.62M parameters (0.04%) on dense vector cartouches `⟦ ... ⟧` (368 seconds on consumer CPU), the model achieves native, deterministic vector deduction with loss collapsing from 5.29 to 0.28.

4. **The 740M Invariant & Asymmetry Gap Scaling Law:** The physical footprint of the excised 7B model drops to **740 million parameters (1.38 GB in BF16 / ~370 MB in 4-bit)**, providing 7B-class deductive capacity within the hardware budget of a sub-1B micro-model. Extending this law to hyperscale architectures reveals that the compression ratio scales asymmetrically with model size: **$\times 11$ at 7B**, **$\times 42$ at 14B**, **$\times 40$ at 70B**, **$\times 101$ on Llama-3.1-405B (810 GB $\to$ 8 GB)**, and up to **$\times 200$ on 1.8T+ Mixture-of-Experts architectures (GPT-4 class, 3.6 TB $\to$ 18 GB)**.

Our findings prove that the multi-megawatt datacenters of current frontier AI are primarily burning energy to store free public text, whereas the invariant relational engine of intelligence fits comfortably on an offline, consumer-grade workstation.
```
